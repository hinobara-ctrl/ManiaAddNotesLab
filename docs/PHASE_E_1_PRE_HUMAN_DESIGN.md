## Exact relation-link semantics

`exact-block-recurrence.1` groups identical block identities, sorts occurrences by start index and emits only adjacent consecutive links: occurrence 1→2, 2→3, …, n−1→n. Four occurrences therefore mean four occurrences and three emitted links, not six all-pairs links. E.1 will publish occurrence and link counts separately without changing Phase E.
