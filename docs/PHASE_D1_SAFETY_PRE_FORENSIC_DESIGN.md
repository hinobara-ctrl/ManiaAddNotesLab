## Semantic model

The model has three separate layers:

1. `RawGeometryRelation`: an exact mathematical same-lane relation such as an equal head, touching boundary or inclusive intersection.
2. `HardValidityViolation`: a relation or scalar condition that violates the actual placement semantics.
3. `AttributableIntroducedViolation`: a hard violation absent from the causal parent state and connected to a named mutation by provenance.

Raw relations are never treated as violations automatically. Snapshot-only differences never prove a causal mutation.

## Frozen hard-validity predicates

- Tap/tap: a same-lane equal head is illegal; different timestamps do not form an interval relation.
- Tap against LN: a tap is illegal while a previous same-lane LN has `EndBeat >= TapBeat`; equality at release is therefore illegal for the tap path.
- LN/LN: overlap uses strict crossing (`previous.EndBeat > candidate.StartBeat` or `candidate.EndBeat > next.StartBeat`). Boundary equality is not overlap.
- LN spacing: the required gap is a separate mutation-specific predicate; it is not derivable from a final raw intersection count.
- LN duration: duration must be positive both in beat space and after millisecond materialization.
- Articulation: each segment is checked while ignoring only its explicitly identified parent. Segment clearance and parent replacement remain distinct from ordinary placement.
- Different lanes cannot form a pairwise geometry violation.

## States and causal units

- `SourceState`: parsed original `.osu`.
- `ControlState`: result generated by `legacy-experimental.1`.
- `TreatmentState`: result of a separately authorized future treatment.
- `RepositoryEntryHead`: Git HEAD before implementation.
- `ImplementationSnapshotHash`: deterministic content identity of the uncommitted implementation used by an experiment.

A direct treatment violation is caused by the treatment-governed mutation itself. A downstream violation is caused by a later mutation whose causal parent state differs because of an earlier treatment decision. Legacy and articulation mutations retain separate causes. Direct/downstream classification requires mutation provenance; final outputs alone produce `Unattributable` where that proof is absent.

## Serialization
